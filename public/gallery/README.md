# Gallery (Render Wall)

Images and short looping videos for the infinite draggable wall on the homepage.

- Images: `.webp`, about 1600px on the long side. Cells are small, the focus card is at most ~900px wide
- Videos: `.webm` or `.mp4`, a few seconds and about 1MB. They play muted on a loop, and every copy on the wall plays at once, so keep them small and few
- Names: lowercase with dashes, e.g. `caramel-pour.webp`
- After adding a file, add it to `renderWall` in `lib/content.ts` with a title, tag and one line, or upload it from the dashboard

Aim for 25 to 30 frames so the wall does not repeat too often.
